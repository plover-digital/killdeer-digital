killdeer.digital

Linux VMs. Managed over SSH.

We’re a small, independent team in Minneapolis building our own hosting stack. Create and manage your virtual machines from your terminal.

Get started

Email your preferred username and SSH public key to machines@plover.digital. New accounts receive $20 credit.

Once your account is ready, create your first VM:

$ssh robin@killdeer.digital create test-vm micro alpine-3.23 --ipv6

Replace robin with your username. This example creates an IPv6-only Micro VM running Alpine Linux. All commands.
